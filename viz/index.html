The Tapestry

A vast field of dark threads — every human life — into which a single golden thread enters at AD 30 and, over two thousand years, kindles light from one to billions, dimming where it is resisted and rekindling after.

Each thread is one sampled human life. The picture is a real agent-based simulation: the seed (Christ, Judaea) is the only life lit at the start; belief spreads only where a lit life touches a dark one, or where a named missionary ignites a place. Every gold thread traces, through who-lit-whom, back to the one seed. The horizontal axis is time; the vertical axis is distance from Judaea — the Latin West fans up, the Orthodox East and Global South fan down.

Gold = practicing · gray-gold = nominal / fraying · dark = unreached or fallen away. ~300,000 sampled lives stand for the billions. Region levels are calibrated to sourced historical anchors; some quantities (per-place detail, incumbency, the practicing/nominal split) are modeled estimates — see the repository's BLOCKERS and calibration report.
